# Lithium Battery Recycling

As electric vehicles, electronics, and energy storage systems multiply across India, lithium battery recycling has become a critical industrial need. Ni-De Tech LLP partners with Eco Recycling S.r.L., Italy — holder of multiple European recycling patents — to bring proven, science-backed recovery methods to Indian industries.

## Precision-Driven Extraction Methods
Combining mechanical dismantling with hydrometallurgical refinement to isolate cobalt, lithium, and nickel from spent battery cells accurately.

## Reducing Industrial Hazard and Waste
Diverting toxic battery components away from landfills while recovering usable metals for reintroduction into manufacturing supply chains.

## Backed by Patented European Technology
Eco Recycling's patented processes, refined over years of R&D, guide every step of our lithium battery recycling operations in India.

## Preparing India for Battery Volume Growth
Anticipating rising demand, Ni-De Tech's engineers are scaling compliant recycling capacity ahead of India's fast-growing battery disposal needs.
